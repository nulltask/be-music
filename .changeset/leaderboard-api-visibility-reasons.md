Say why a play is not public. A play only its own player sees in the lists is marked "not public" (or "hidden") with a help tip listing the reasons: its chart is not listed yet, it was played on an anonymous account (with how to make it public), or the player hid it. Before, the reason was only in a hover title. A play's page shows the same reasons in a notice, and the Help page gains a "Who sees a play" section.
