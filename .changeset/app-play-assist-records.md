Assisted plays no longer raise local best scores. Each play is recorded by its judge ruleset's assist rule: an assisted play moves only its limited lamp and its play count, LR2's D-BATTLE, SP TO DP and EXTRA MODE keep a lamp of their own, a `no-save` play (That-Which-Must-Not-Be-Named's BATTLE) keeps nothing, and an assisted course stage or run leaves the records alone. A play given up with ESC is recorded once its replay is ready, so the same rule applies to it. Records stored before this change keep their values.
