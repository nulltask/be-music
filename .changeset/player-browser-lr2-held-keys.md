LR2's held keys on the select screen. Key 6 starts the chart in AUTO as it comes up, or plays its saved replay when held for 2 s (`REPLAY_HOLD_MS`). Key 4 backs out on a tap and asks the host for the chart's ranking when held (`SelectSessionEvents.ranking`, `RANKING_HOLD_MS`). F3 held turns the arrows to LR2's level edit (`SelectSessionEvents.levelEdit`: ↑ / ↓ the difficulty, ← / → the level). The key table adds `global.help` (LR2's F1).
