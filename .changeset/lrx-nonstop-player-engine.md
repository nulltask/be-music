A NONSTOP course of LRX stages stitches into an LRX chart that keeps each note's sound values, chain notes and hidden notes, with its long notes as CNs. A course that mixes LRX with BMS or bmson, or LRX stages of different lane types, is refused (`lrx-mixed`, `lrx-layout-mismatch`).
