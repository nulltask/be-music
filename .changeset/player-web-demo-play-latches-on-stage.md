A play now puts what it was made with in force as it takes the stage rather than as it is made: the start that R on its result plays again (and the test hook reports), the record keys its result is kept under, and its beatoraja play entry. A play loads behind the decide screen, so a decide screen backed out of no longer leaves them pointing at the play it was loading. The beatoraja play entry belongs to its play, so a play-skin pick made in the Debug Menu while a decide screen is up is now kept for the next play, as a pick for any screen not showing is, rather than re-skinning the play loading behind it.
