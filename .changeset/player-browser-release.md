Add the LR2 mania options, AC and beatoraja display options, score targets and GUIDE SE, render with WebGL 2 by default, and follow LR2's lane options and each NONSTOP stage's rules more closely.

- Dim the lanes under the notes by `laneBrightness`, and draw no judgment or combo while `judgeDisplay` is `OFF`, on the built-in, LR2 and beatoraja play screens.
- Add the select-screen operations **BATTLE (????)** (numpad 7), **M-RAN 1P** (numpad 6) and **M-RAN 2P** (Shift + numpad 6) to the key table, as AC 34's numpad sets them.
- Skip drawing the notes of the scratch lanes AUTO SCRATCH plays while `autoScratchNotes` is `HIDE`, on the built-in, LR2 and beatoraja playfields. The notes still play and count.
- Draw beatoraja's judge area (the judge windows as coloured bands above the judge line) while `showJudgeArea` is on, and draw a judged tap note with the skin's processed-note image, or dimmed, while `markProcessedNotes` is on. Both work on the built-in, LR2 and beatoraja playfields.
- The LR2 and beatoraja result screens scroll a course run's stage list when it has more stages than the skin's rows (LR2's five, beatoraja's ten): one stage up every two seconds until the last is on the bottom row, then from stage 1 again, so every stage is shown.
- Play beatoraja's GUIDE SE: with `PlayOptions.guideSE` on, a play decodes the sound set's guide sounds (`AudioService.decodeSound`) and its audio session plays the judgment's on the key bus.
- Synesthesia's play screen holds a steady frame rate on laptops with integrated graphics: on an Intel UHD laptop (WebGPU, full effects, a chart without BGA) it went from about 88 to 120–137 fps at 1280 × 720, with frames over 16.7 ms down from one in seven to under one in fifty, and from 46 to about 80 fps at 1920 × 1080.
  - `PixiSceneHost.mount` takes a `renderer` option (`'webgl'` or `'webgpu'`) and renders with WebGL 2 when none is given, where it used to prefer WebGPU: on that laptop WebGPU drew the play screen at 1920 × 1080 at about 80 fps against 135 fps with WebGL 2. `?renderer=webgl` and `?renderer=webgpu` override it, and `resolveRendererPreference` takes the requested renderer as its second argument.
  - Synesthesia has a "Frosted glass" setting. Its frosted panels capture and blur the backdrop every frame, a large share of the frame on an integrated GPU, so they are on by default only on macOS; elsewhere the panels are plain dark fills unless the player turns the setting on.
  - The texture a Pixi skin draws into is no longer 4x multisampled. Points smaller than a device pixel grow to one pixel and fade by the area they gain, so the dust keeps its fine, soft look.
  - The idle monitor's charge cloud draws its particles as indexed quads in one draw instead of one four-vertex instance each, which left most of an integrated GPU's vertex lanes idle.
  - The charge cloud simulates about 30 steps a second whatever the display's rate, its particles carried on along their velocity between steps, and works out its camera's angles once a frame instead of for every vertex.
  - A key press tints the sky by mixing into the ground's colours instead of filling it a second time.
  - The idle monitor no longer moves or hides its cloud every frame, which made Pixi rebuild the whole background layer's draw list.
- Show the judge detail (FAST / SLOW or ±ms, the keys and the scratch apart on request) above the judgment on the built-in skins, and hide their judgment counts when the player turns them off.
- Move the built-in skins' judgment and combo by the `judgePosition` play option, and move the BPM change warning with them so the two stay as far apart as the skin placed them.
- Display-only play options changed during a play take effect at once: the new `display-options` play intent carries the auto scratch note display, the LR2 mania drawing options, the judge area, mark processed notes and the score target to the running play.
- Pass the `lr2Gambol` play option to the engine under LR2 rules; a replay plays back with its recorded level.
- Build LR2's mania chart generators into the play's chart, and rebuild a recorded play from its rates and seed, failing when the rebuilt counts differ.
- Draw LR2's mania drawing options on the built-in and LR2 playfields (EARTHQUAKE on beatoraja skins too): ACCELERATION, EARTHQUAKE, TORNADO, SUPERLOOP, CHAR, HEARTBEAT, NABEATSU, SINCURVE, WAVE, SPIRAL effect, SIDEJUMP. They follow LR2's formulas in its 640x480 pixels, scaled to the stage, and change neither note times nor judgment.
- LR2's S-RANDOM and CONVERGE on a nine-key chart can put a note on the 2P scratch, outside the playfield, and LR2's CONVERGE puts its 2P target one lane to the right (key 0 is key 1, the last key points past the keys), as LR2 does. S-RANDOM 9KEY corrected keeps to the nine keys, and CONVERGE corrected does both corrections.
- Play a recorded SOFTLANDING back from its scroll timeline, and reject a recording without one.
- Add the select-screen operation **LR2 mania options** (`select.mania-options`, F2 on the LR2 key map, unbound on beatoraja's where F2 reloads the folders) and the `SelectSessionEvents.maniaOptions` callback a host shows them from.
- RANDOM draws the same arrangement under every source and ruleset, as beatoraja's lane shuffle does, so a seed gives beatoraja's arrangement; LR2's fixed key then moves the original key 1 to the chosen key under every ruleset. AC's S-RANDOM runs LR2's, as before, and a play option mismatch names it S-RANDOM (LR2 / ????).
- Show the song being played in a NONSTOP play: the built-in skins (Plain and Synesthesia) used to show the course's title for the whole run. They now show each stage's title, artist and genre, crossfading over 0.6 s as the next stage begins. LR2 and beatoraja skins keep their own rules.
- Speed up stitching a NONSTOP course from a large library. `createNonstopAssets` built a reverse index over every file of each stage's source to name a found file, which on a library of hundreds of thousands of files made the stitch take most of a second and froze the page; it now takes the key the lookup found the file under. A WAV's codec and folder fallbacks are collected in one pass instead of one lookup chain per fallback.
  - New `resolveChartAudioAssetCandidates`: every file `resolveChartAudioAsset` can play for a path, best first, each with its key in the source.
  - `resolveChartBGAAsset` also returns the `key` of the file it found.
  - `createNonstopAssets` returns `prefetch()`, which looks up every WAV / BMP the stage charts declare ahead of the stitch, yielding to the page between slices, and `setStageCharts()`, which hands over the stage charts as played.
- Add `LoadProgressOptions.parsedChartCache`, a `ParsedChartCache` a host gives the chart collection loader to keep parsed charts across loads: the loader looks each chart file up by the SHA-256 of its bytes and the kind of its name (`parsedChartCacheKey`), parses only the charts it does not find, and keeps those. `parseChartThroughCache`, `parsedChartCacheKey` and `parsedChartPathKind` are exported with it. A cache that fails to read or write leaves the load parsing as before.
- `CourseMaker.saveRandomMix` saves a RANDOM MIX the player has played as a course of their own under a name: the kept draw is renamed and no longer replaced by the next draw, and a draw that was not kept (an endless run's) joins the saved courses as the newest of its kind.
- Add the result operations **Retry (same arrangement)** (`result.retry-same`, T; beatoraja's key 7 held as the screen leaves) and **Gauge graph** (`result.change-graph`, G; beatoraja's key 6). `on.retry` now says when the same arrangement was asked for, `ResultSceneOptions.gaugeGraphs` supplies the graphs to step through, and a beatoraja skin's graphs redraw when their line changes without growing.
- A play without a ghost pacemaker now paces against the fixed target of `scoreTarget`: the frame's score targets carry its EX score and name to every skin family.
- Show a ranking score target once the host gives its score (`PlaySceneOptions.rankingTarget`), follow the score target's rate live, and draw a fixed target on LR2 skins: the target score and difference (numbers 151 and 153) and the target bar graphs (14 and 15) pace with the notes judged.
- The BPM change warning lost the right end of its text when the number of digits changed (`99 » 1000` after `60 » 240`) on Canvas skins: its figures are now drawn as tabular glyphs, sharp at the size the canvas is shown at. The built-in skins draw the box in their own type and their own blue and red, and the warning fades out after the change instead of cutting off.
- Play chart BASE-SPEED above the player slider limit, including audio stretching and BGA playback at the effective rate.
- Fix the course result of the built-in skins (Synesthesia and Plain) squeezing a long course's stages together to fit the card: every stage keeps its usual height with its artist, and the stages past the card scroll up into view, and the list fades out at the end and back in at stage 1 to start over.
- Fix the course result of the built-in skins (Synesthesia and Plain) naming only the course and the first stage's artist, level and BPM in its track column. The result of an EXPERT, GRADE (dan) or NONSTOP run now lists every stage chart of the course in order, with its title, level and artist, and how many stages the run reached; a stage the run never reached is dimmed. A single play's and a course stage's results are unchanged.
- Place and clip charatbeatHDX BGA images and videos in the skin viewport, including per-stage NONSTOP crossfades.
- The sparks and embers rising from a held long note no longer cover its end: Synesthesia and Plain keep them under the note's end and inside its width, so the end the player lets go at stays easy to see. The player fills each held lane's `holdTop` for Be-Music Skins.
- Fix frame drops on slower machines by cutting the play screen's per-frame work. With the CPU throttled to a sixth of its speed, the built-in Synesthesia skin used to fall to about 40 fps and now holds 60 fps; at a quarter of its speed it went from 88 % to 53 % of the main thread and from 186 MB to 97 MB of garbage a second.
  - Skin points (Synesthesia's floor, dust and powder, some 16,000 a frame) draw as instanced quads from one typed array per layer, 16 bytes a point, instead of Pixi particles rebuilt by a generated upload loop that also boxed doubles onto the global object.
  - The floor lattice works out its projection inline, the bomb mask is redrawn only when the lanes move, and labels, styles and gauge beads are reused instead of rebuilt.
  - The chrome, overlay and bomb layers are their own Pixi render groups, so a change in one no longer re-collects the whole screen's instructions.
  - Reticle arms use square caps instead of round ones, which Pixi rebuilt as fans of segments every frame.
  - The display-area readout, chart text and BPM-change warning no longer force a layout every frame on DOM skins.
  - The built-in chrome's per-frame runtime keeps one shape, so skins reading it stay monomorphic.
- Apply LR2 beta3’s order-dependent scratch avoidance during 14 KEY chart preparation for LR2 S-RANDOM and CONVERGE.
- Fix LR2 two-player BATTLE leaving the 2P player without mines when both players pick the same lane option, as LR2 copies 1P's arranged lanes mines included. Replays rebuild the 2P mines only when they recorded them.
- The corrected LR2 HAZARD gauge (`DEATH-CORRECTED`) starts full before the play, like the other survival gauges, and LR2 select skins step from it to EASY, as from the HAZARD cell they show it on.
- Fix a BMS stage of a NONSTOP course stitched as bmson playing its keysounds polyphonically and drawing its layer images' black opaque, and pass each stage's `#WAV00` to the engine so NONSTOP mines sound their explosion.
