`resolveLanePlayLabel` now returns `PRACTICE PLAY` for a practice play the player plays, so a lane bank shows the mode where AUTO PLAY and GHOST PLAY appear; a ghost and AUTO PLAY still take precedence. The new `resolvePlayModeLabel` names the play mode for a header: `AUTO PLAY`, `PRACTICE PLAY` or `PLAY`.
