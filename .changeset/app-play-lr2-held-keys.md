LR2's select screen keys: key 4 held opens the chart's ranking on the Leaderboard (LR2's IR), F3 held with the arrows edits the chart's difficulty and level (kept in this browser and put over the chart each time the library is read), key 6 held for 2 s plays the chart's saved replay, and F1 opens Help on LR2 and built-in skins.
