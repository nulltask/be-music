The replay ghost can now be compared in three ways and from four sources. Debug Menu → Play options → Replay / ghost → **Ghost** picks the replay (OFF, OWN BEST, REPLAY SLOT 1-4, LEADERBOARD TOP or REPLAY FILE) and the new **Ghost mode** picks how it shows: SECOND PLAYFIELD (as before), PACEMAKER (the ghost's EX score at the notes you have judged as the score graph's pacemaker and the target numbers, on every skin family), or PACEMAKER + LANES (the pacemaker with the ghost's key presses and judgments over your own lanes on the built-in skins). **Ghost status** says whether the chart under the cursor shows the pick, and **Ghost replay file…** picks the file REPLAY FILE plays. LEADERBOARD TOP plays the replay of the chart's top run under your ruleset.
