LR2's S-RANDOM and CONVERGE on a nine-key chart can put a note on the 2P scratch, outside the playfield, and LR2's CONVERGE puts its 2P target one lane to the right (key 0 is key 1, the last key points past the keys), as LR2 does. S-RANDOM 9KEY corrected keeps to the nine keys, and CONVERGE corrected does both corrections.
