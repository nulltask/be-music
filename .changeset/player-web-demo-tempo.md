Play a chart from -50 % to +50 % of its tempo on every skin family, from the Debug Menu's Tempo folder: Tempo (%), Pitch (Pitch follows tempo, as LR2, by default, or Keep pitch, which also keeps a NONSTOP FIT stage's pitch) and Keep-pitch stretcher (Signalsmith Stretch by default, or WSOLA). The settings are remembered and the status line shows a tempo other than 0. With the pitch following the tempo, the slider changes a running play's tempo live; with Keep pitch, during a replay and on a course stage that turns the tempo off, it stays locked. A play or course run at another tempo at any point is practice: it is not sent to the Leaderboard and leaves the best records alone, while its play log is still saved. A replay plays at its recorded tempo.
