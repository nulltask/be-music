Keep up to four replays per chart and per course in the browser, as beatoraja does: save a play into a slot on its result screen (keys 1-4, a beatoraja skin's replay buttons, or the replay-slot card on the built-in, Native and LR2 skins), give each slot an auto-save rule in Debug Menu → Library & records → Replay slots, and replay a slot from the select screen. Replays are stored gzip-compressed in IndexedDB under a 500 MB budget; past it the lowest-scoring replays are removed first.
