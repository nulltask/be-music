Lane keys can be set by the player: `LaneModeOptions.laneKeys` (and the same option on `preparePlaybackChart` and `applySevenToNine`) gives lanes their own input tokens over the defaults, for the 5 / 7 / 10 / 14 KEY layouts by channel and the 9 KEY layouts by key number. `LANE_KEY_CONFIG` lists each group's lanes with their default keys, and `laneKeyLabel` names a key from its token.
