New play option Debug Menu → Play options → **ANY KEY (PC)** (off by default, shown with the ???? judge rules only): whichever key you press takes the nearest note on any lane, the scratch included, and a long note stays held until that key is released. As on the PC edition, a play with it saves no record and is stored unranked on the Leaderboard. It is disabled during a play, and a replay plays back with the switch it recorded.
