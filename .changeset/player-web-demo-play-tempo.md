Add Debug Menu → Play options → **Tempo (%)**, which plays a song from -50 % to +50 % of its tempo on every skin family, with **Tempo: pitch** and **Tempo: keep-pitch stretcher** deciding how it sounds (also for NONSTOP FIT stages). The three settings are remembered, the status line shows a tempo other than 0, and a replay plays at its recorded tempo. A play or course run at another tempo is practice: it is not sent to the Leaderboard (the status line says why) and leaves the best records alone; its play log is still auto-saved.

Disable every play option that is read when a play starts while a play runs, now including **Course: NONSTOP placement** and the tempo settings.
