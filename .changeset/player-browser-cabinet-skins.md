Lay the built-in skins' landscape play screen out as a cabinet: the keys, gauge, score and hi-speed under the lanes, a score graph against the pacemaker and the best record beside them, and the stage, players, BGA, judgement panel, BPM and tempo on the right. A double-play chart is centred, with the chart, BGA and judgement panel on its left and the graph on its right.
Add `playerName` to `PlaySceneOptions`, and hand the built-in skins the best record, course stage, player name, lane option and tempo.
