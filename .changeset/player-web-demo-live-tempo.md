With the pitch following the tempo, the Debug Menu's Tempo (%) slider stays enabled during a play and changes its tempo live; the status line follows, and a play that ran at another tempo at any point leaves the records alone (a course run too). With Keep pitch, during a replay and on a course stage the course turned the tempo off for, the slider stays locked as before.
