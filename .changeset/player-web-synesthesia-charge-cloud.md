Synesthesia's audio orb is now a charge cloud: five small dark orbs, each lit by its own slice of the spectrum (a one-frame flash with light shafts, then a glow that fades over about a second), hang in a GPU-simulated fluid of up to ~150,000 particles. The particles are drawn to or driven off the orbs by charge, with each particle tuned to one spectrum band, and flock through a coarse velocity/density grid so the cloud folds into smoky sheets lit pink to white near a flaring orb and deep indigo elsewhere. The select screen's foreground orb and the gameplay idle monitor use it; the old particle shell, its fibres, the smaller background orb and the black moon are gone.
