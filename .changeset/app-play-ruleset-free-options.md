The Debug Menu offers every lane and battle option under every judge ruleset, grouped by source with the ruleset's own first: RANDOM, S-RANDOM and CONVERGE once per source (RANDOM (LR2), RANDOM (beatoraja), ...), BATTLE (LR2) two-player play also under beatoraja rules, and BATTLE (beatoraja) and BATTLE (????) apart. ADD LN / CN / HCN / ALL and their add rate are offered under every ruleset. Saved settings with a bare RANDOM, S-RANDOM, CONVERGE or BATTLE are read as the qualified value of the ruleset they were saved with.
