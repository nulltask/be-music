beatoraja skins' select screens now have beatoraja's option panels: START opens panel 1, SELECT panel 2, and START and SELECT together (or 5) panel 3, shown while held or kept open on a tap. The skin draws them on its panel timers (21-23 open, 31-33 close) and `OPTION_PANEL1`-`3`. Their 1P keys step the options beatoraja's do: panel 1 RANDOM, the gauge, the DP option, HS-FIX and the 2P RANDOM; panel 2 CUSTOM JUDGE, CONSTANT, the judge area display, the LN mode, the BPM guide and the mines; panel 3 the BGA, the gauge auto-shift, the judge timing auto-adjust, and the visible time and judge timing by 1 ms.
