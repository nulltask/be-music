A `.bme` chart without a 6 / 7 key column is now played as 5 / 10 KEY instead of being forced to 7 / 14 KEY: the lane mode comes from the lanes the chart uses, as in beatoraja, and only `.pms` still names a mode through `laneModeExtension`. Under the beatoraja ruleset such a chart now also gets the 5 KEY judge windows.
