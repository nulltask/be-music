`BeMusicLongNote.backSpinScratch` marks a back-spin scratch, and new helpers tell the long-note kinds apart by shape rather than colour: `resolveLongNoteMarks`, `isBackSpinScratchLongNote`, `hellChargeRungs` (an HCN's body rungs, flowing while held) and `backSpinChevron` (the chevron on a BSS's tail cap).
