`BeMusicLongNote.backSpinScratch` marks a back-spin scratch, and new helpers tell the long-note kinds apart by shape rather than colour: `resolveLongNoteMarks`, `isBackSpinScratchLongNote`, `hellChargeZigzag` (an HCN's zigzag centre line, as a polyline that can flow either way) and `backSpinChevron` (the chevron on a BSS's tail cap).
