Add a desktop app that wraps the web player in Electron. Its window opens the player from its server, keeps the sign-in flow inside and other pages in the default browser, grants the player's permission requests without prompts, keeps full rate behind other windows, remembers its placement, and works offline once the player has been opened online. The macOS build is signed ad hoc and the Windows installer is unsigned.
