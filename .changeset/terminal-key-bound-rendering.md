Render engine-selected SWBGA frames in ANSI and Kitty output, including BMP00, placed aliases, EXBMP resources and keyed video frames. Accept optional input-bound frame state in the renderer, preserve normal BGA resources and restore the underlying planes after release or expiry. NONSTOP SWBGA resource remapping remains unsupported.
