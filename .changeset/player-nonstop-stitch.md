Stitch LR2 NONSTOP courses: `stitchNonstopCourse` turns the placed stages into one BMS chart that plays as LR2's NONSTOP loader builds it — a time-based 150-BPM scroll with every object at its stage's own time, each stage's bar lines and STOPs in its window, LR2's seam rule turning overlapping notes into background sound, and keys renumbered into one base-62 space — with a map of each stage's keys, window, sound fades and BGA crossfade for the host.
