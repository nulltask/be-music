The Options dialog offers the `plugout` judge ruleset and its gauges under plugout's own names (NORMAL, HAZARD, EXNORMAL, EXHARD, EXHAZARD, EX EASY), also as another source's gauges under every other ruleset. Plays judged under plugout keep their best records and dan ranks, and its lowest auto-shift gauge reads EASY / NORMAL.
