The built-in mine's caution stripes no longer spill out of the mine into the neighbouring lanes. Mine notes now reach be-music skins that draw them (`gameplay.drawsMines`), and the built-in Pixi skins draw them through an optional `renderMine`, falling back to the caution-striped bar.

Synesthesia draws its own mines: note-shaped but dark crimson, with a broken filament that flickers where a note carries its clean white one.
