Keep local best records per judge ruleset. Plays under LR2, beatoraja and IIDX folded into one record per chart and course, although each ruleset has its own lamps, BP and scoring: a beatoraja MAX and an LR2 FULL COMBO could not be told apart. A play now records into the ruleset it ran under — single plays, EXPERT / GRADE stages folded into their charts' records (now lamped under that ruleset on beatoraja skins too) and course runs (a run whose stages were judged under different rulesets keeps none) — and the select screen shows the current ruleset's records, switching when Judge windows changes. Records kept before this are read as LR2 records, the default ruleset.
