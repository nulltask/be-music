Keep the best records of beatoraja plays like those of LR2 and default plays. A beatoraja play's lamp, EX score, max combo and miss count now go into the saved records under the same rules (not for auto play, replays, course stages or another tempo), and the beatoraja select lights each bar's lamp from those records, so lamps survive a reload and show plays from any skin family. Before, beatoraja plays were never recorded and their select lamps lasted only for the session.
