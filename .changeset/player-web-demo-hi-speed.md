Remember the hi-speed across plays and reloads, and pick it with the Debug Menu's Hi-speed slider. A change made during a play with the arrow keys, on the LR2 select panel or on the slider is kept for the next play, and the slider also applies to a running play. It used to reset to 2.0× on every reload, and a beatoraja play always started at 1.0×.
