New play option Debug Menu → Play options → Replay / ghost → **Ghost** (OFF / OWN BEST, off by default): OWN BEST replays your best on the chart (its replay slot with the highest EX score) as a second playfield beside the play on the built-in skins. It is disabled during a play and wherever the chart under the cursor shows none (no replay kept, the LR2 or beatoraja skins, a course, a battle mode, or a chart other than 5 / 7 KEY single play), and it never touches your records, replays or Leaderboard submission.
