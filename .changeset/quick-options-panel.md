A Quick options panel opens and closes at the stage's bottom right on the select and play screens, with the options changed most: Random 1P, Gauge, Auto play, HS-FIX, Hi-speed, Effect 1P and Cover height. It sets the same values as the Options dialog; the ones a play reads as it starts are disabled during one, and the hi-speed and covers change a running play live.
