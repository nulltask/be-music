ESC on a course stage played on LR2's course gauges goes on to the next stage while the gauge holds, as LR2 does, instead of ending the course; the other gauges still show the course result. Once the stage judged a GOOD or better, its result shows as FAILED before the next stage or the course result, as in LR2 (Skip stage results still skips it), and the stage is recorded as given up with no stage record. A course replay plays on past a stage its run gave up and went on after, showing its result where the recorded run did.
