Serve Leaderboard pages under `/leaderboard`: the newest plays as the top page, a score under every ruleset, a chart's rankings with ruleset tabs and filters, and a player's plays. A chart is now listed publicly once two non-anonymous players have played it; the top page shows every play by a non-anonymous player, tagging charts that are not listed yet as new, and anonymous plays on request.
