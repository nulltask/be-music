Serve the Leaderboard pages under `/leaderboard`, in English and Japanese, styled with Tailwind CSS v4 and Basecoat in the system colour scheme:

- Latest: the newest plays, each with the chart's mode and level, the EX-Score gained over the player's previous best, a raised lamp, fewer misses, the lane option and the play's place on the chart.
- Chart: the rankings under ruleset tabs, listing every play or a player's best and narrowing to one chart file, with the chart's details (mode, level and difficulty, TOTAL, judge rank, long-note mode where the chart has long notes, format, `#RANDOM` outcomes seen, file hashes), its play and player counts and the spread of clear lamps, its chartset in the breadcrumbs, and links to Mocha-Repository, MinIR, BMS Search and the LR2IR archive by file hash.
- Chartset, player and score pages: a work's charts; a player's plays and figures (play time, notes played with every judgment, average rate, the lamp and DJ Rank spreads, the rulesets played under); a play's score under every ruleset with everything its info popover shows, a hide / show button for its player and a Share section (X with `#bemusic`, the system share sheet, copy link).
- Share cards: a score page previews as a 1200 × 630 card on X, Discord and other link previews, drawn by the player's browser on their first visit (`PUT /api/v0/scores/:id/card`, owner only): the chart's densest stretch on the playfield as the Synesthesia skin shows it at HIGH SPEED 2.0, the DJ Rank, EX-Score and rate, lamp, judgments and player in the lamp's colour, kept clear of the overlays link previews lay over a card.
- Who sees what: a chart is listed publicly once two players with an account have played it. Until then its plays show only to their players, tagged "not listed" with the reason, its ranking shows a signed-in player only their own record, its figures are hidden and its pages carry `noindex`. A play that is not public (an unlisted chart, an anonymous account, a hidden play) is marked with the reasons in a help tip and on its page.
- Every page shows its figures under one display ruleset chosen in the header (kept in a cookie). The language comes from `?lang=`, then the language menu (kept in a cookie through `/leaderboard/lang/:locale`), then `Accept-Language`; game terms stay as players use them.
- An About page (`/leaderboard/about`) tells what sets the Leaderboard apart and how plays are scored, charts are known and accounts work; a Help page (`/leaderboard/help`) has the lamp legend and the terms, linked from small "?" tips wherever a switch or term needs them.
- Compact lists that never scroll sideways on a phone and lay out against their own width, times in the viewer's local time (list times as how long ago), a header with the breadcrumb trail as its second row, icons that tell charts, chartsets, players and scores apart, and a site icon with a web app manifest (`pnpm run build:icons` renders the PNGs from `src/pages/icons/icon.svg`).
