On a beatoraja theme, a NONSTOP course and a play-log replay fell back to the built-in play screen (and the NONSTOP result to the built-in result with the theme's jingles). Both now play on the theme's own play skin, as every other play does: the play session carries the stitched chart's per-stage audio and a replay's arrangement and view for every family. A NONSTOP run shows its result on the theme's result skin, with the clear / fail jingle, the stitched chart for its graphs, and R stitching and playing the course again; a theme without a result skin still falls back to the built-in result.
