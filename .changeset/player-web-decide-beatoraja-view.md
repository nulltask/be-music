`createDecideScene` takes a beatoraja kit (`{ family: 'beatoraja', kit }`, a theme's decide entry loaded with `loadBeatorajaSceneKit`) as well as a be-music or an LR2 one. The beatoraja family's decide view draws the decide skin through `BeatorajaPlaySkinView` from the decide session's frames: the chart's texts, level and BPM, its difficulty ops and the loading bar through the scene mapping (`resolveBeatorajaDecide*` of `@be-music/beatoraja-skin/scene`), the BPM and note-kind graphs of the chart as the play will play it (`DecideFrame.playChart`, the session's `#RANDOM` draws), the chart's own images (loaded from `source` before the first frame) and beatoraja's scene timers on the screen's own clock. The screen keeps the beatoraja decide screen's timing: input after the skin's `input` (500 ms without one), the move to the play after its `scene` (3000 ms; `scene = 0` waits for the player) and the fade-out over its `fadeout` (500 ms), with Escape held back until input opens. The skin's Lua sounds play through the audio service's skin sounds, owned by the screen. `DecideScene.updateKit` ignores a kit of another family or skin.

`PixiBeatorajaDecideScene` and its `PixiBeatorajaDecideSceneOptions` type are removed; the beatoraja decide screen is made with `createDecideScene`. The screen now takes its keys as the decide session does: an auto-repeated Enter no longer continues (a key held down from the select screen does not skip the screen once `input` has passed, as on beatoraja), and a key typed into a text field is the field's. Its decide sound plays on the audio service's shared context and stops through `stopOwned` as the screen leaves, as it stopped with the scene's own context before; the skin's Lua sounds stop then too.

Migration:

- `new PixiBeatorajaDecideScene({ skin, textures, fonts, skinConfig, song, chart, chartImages, bgmBytes, skinAudio, onContinue, onCancel })` → `createDecideScene({ family: 'beatoraja', kit }, { chart: song, source, sounds: { decideBgm: { bytes: bgmBytes } }, audio, on: { continue: onContinue, cancel: onCancel } })`, where `kit` is `loadBeatorajaSceneKit`'s, `source` the chart's files (the scene loads `#STAGEFILE`, `#BACKBMP` and `#BANNER` itself), and `audio` the host's `createAudioService()` with the theme's files set (`audio.setTheme({ files })`) for the skin's Lua sounds.
- The chart the screen draws its graphs from is the session's own draw of the chart's `#RANDOM` branches: hand `scene.resolved` to the play instead of resolving the chart before the screen.
- `autoAdvanceMs` and `getLoadingProgress` have no counterpart: the screen moves on after the skin's `scene`, and `progress` (how far the play behind the screen has loaded) replaces the loading callback.
