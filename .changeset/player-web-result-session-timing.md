The result session follows two more of the skin's choices. Its view's timing (`ResultViewTiming`) can say the screen has no steps to press through (`skipsResultSteps`), so the first press leaves; LR2 and built-in screens still skip the graphs, then show the record update, then leave. It can also hold Escape back until input opens (`SceneViewTiming.escapeWaitsForInput`). Both are as on the beatoraja result screen. `ResultFrame.playChart` carries the chart as the play played it, when the host gives it, for a skin's chart graphs.
