The PRACTICE bar picker groups its settings into Tempo, Range and Gauge and judge, sets the tempo from a **Target BPM** (the BPM the bars picked start at, at the play tempo), and offers **Keep LNs held into the bars** (on by default), also on the Options dialog's Practice page. With **Keep pitch**, the picker stretches each bar's sounds in the background while it is open and starts over when the tempo or pitch changes, so bars sound without the wait and the page no longer stalls.
