Add "Auto-detect resolution" to the Debug Menu's Skin & display folder. It measures the frame rate on the screen being shown at 1× and then at each higher resolution from the top down, and keeps the highest that holds the 1× frame rate. A device whose CPU, not its resolution, limits the frame rate keeps its highest resolution. The pick is stored like a manual one.
