Synesthesia and Plain show how a hell charge note stands: a violet HELL colour with a still zigzag centre line before it is reached, a bright pulsing orange glow with the zigzag flowing into the judgement line while held, a dim red with the zigzag flowing back up the lane once let go, and the glow again when pressed back, ending at the tail.
