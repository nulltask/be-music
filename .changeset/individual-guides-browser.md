Guide labels show by kind on their own: the BPM labels follow `bpmGuide`, and `bpmGuideKinds` turns the STOP, `#SCROLL` and `#SPEED` labels on whether or not `bpmGuide` is on (`resolveShownGuideKinds`). Without `bpmGuideKinds`, STOPs follow `bpmGuide` as beatoraja's guide draws them.
