Options → Skin → BGA camera shows a camera in the play's BGA frame instead of the chart's BGA, to film your hands on the keys or your face for a stream. Pick any camera (Allow camera access names them), mirror it, and switch it during a play. The camera opens only during a play with the setting on, closes on every other screen, and the play falls back to the BGA when the camera is unplugged or its permission is taken back. The picture is not sent anywhere; a Capture recording includes it.
