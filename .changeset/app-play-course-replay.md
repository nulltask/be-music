Replay a whole course from its course replay: dropping a `*.bmcourse.json` (or picking it with Open files) plays the recorded run back as its course once every stage chart is loaded, each stage from its own replay with its recorded carry-in, showing each stage's result and the course result, and stopping where the run stopped. A NONSTOP run plays its stitched replay as one chart. A course replay keeps no record, saves no replay and submits nothing to the Leaderboard, and a dialog lists the stage charts that are missing.
