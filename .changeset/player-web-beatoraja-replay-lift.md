A replay played on the beatoraja play screen can show what the recorded player saw of the LIFT: the play log's covers set the play state's LIFT too on a view that draws one (the beatoraja play view; `PlaylogViewReplay.lift` turns it off), and HIDDEN+ is set over the lane above it, so the cover draws the recorded fraction of the lane. The built-in and LR2 play views draw no LIFT and show the recorded SUDDEN+ and HIDDEN+ as before.
