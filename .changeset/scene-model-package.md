Add `@be-music/scene-model`, the runtime-neutral models of what every screen shows and how it behaves, so the built-in, LR2 and beatoraja skin families share one implementation per screen and only draw. Where the families differed, the models follow LR2, and beatoraja for what LR2 does not have (HS-FIX, LIFT, cover adjustment during play, early / late counts, beatoraja's sorts and 24-key modes); docs/scene-model.md lists each choice.

- Library: the charts, folders and courses a select screen lists, in the glossary's chart wording (`LibraryChart`; `LibraryFolder` with its `charts`, or `courses` for a course folder; `LibraryCourse`, with each stage matched to a local chart, a beatoraja random course's `random`, LR2's NEW COURSE bar `newCourse` and the player's own `editable` courses; `LibraryCourseStage` with its `chart`; `LibraryEntry`, whose chart bar is `{ kind: 'chart', chart }`; `Library` with its `charts`), the root folders (`groupChartsByFolder`, `libraryRootFolders`), a chart's play variant and key mode (`resolveLibraryChartPlayVariant`, `seedLibraryChartPlayVariant`, `resolveLibraryChartKeyMode`) and the key naming a bar across rebuilds (`libraryEntryKey`).
- Browse: the folder stack, cursor, search, sort, filters and favourites as one immutable state (`BrowseState`, `createBrowseState`), changed by commands that report what happened (`reduceBrowse`; backing out of a folder lands on that folder's bar wherever a sort moved it), the list derived from it (`browseEntries`, `listEntriesAt`, `focusedEntry`, `focusedChart`, `focusedCourse`), its rules on their own (`matchesSearchQuery`, `sortLibraryEntries`, `chartMatchesDifficultyFilter`, `chartMatchesKeysFilter`, `courseMatchesKeysFilter`, `wrappedCursorDelta`) and its snapshot, which a select screen keeps across a play (`BrowseSnapshot`, `captureBrowse`, `restoreBrowse`).
- Play options: `PlayOptions` and its value types, `DEFAULT_PLAY_OPTIONS`, each option's choices in LR2 button order (`PLAY_OPTION_CHOICES` and the `*_CYCLE` lists), validation and cycling (`resolvePlayOptionValue`, `resolvePlayOptions`, `cyclePlayOptionValue`, `nextChoice`, `isListPlayOption`) and the hi-speed range (`snapHiSpeed`, `stepPlayerHiSpeed`, `easeHiSpeed`, `HiSpeedTransition`).
- Play state: the running gauge and EX score with their graphs, the combo and a course stage's course combo, the timed judgments with each judgment's early / late count, the hi-speed under HS-FIX and the lane covers with LIFT and HIDDEN, as one immutable `PlayState` fed by the engine's frames and judgments through `reducePlayState` (`createPlayState`, `playEventFromUiCommand`, `recentJudgeTimings`, `createHiSpeedState`, `reduceHiSpeed`, `resolveHsFixMultiplier`, `laneCoversFromPlayOptions`, `reduceLaneCovers`, `resolveLaneCover`), and its view in the play log's units (`resolvePlaylogView`, `resolveWhiteNumber`, `resolveGreenNumber`, `PlaylogViewCursor`, `resolveBeatorajaPlaylogView`). `resolveHiSpeedForWhiteNumber` is the inverse of `resolveWhiteNumber`, so a replay on another hi-speed scale shows the visible time its play log recorded; like the white number, it leaves LIFT out, as beatoraja's `LaneRenderer` does.
- Play result: `PlayResult`, the one result payload for single plays, course stages, course runs and NONSTOP runs (`run`, `course`, `courseRun`), with the gauge's clear border and the TOTAL it ran on (`gaugeBorder`, `gaugeTotal`) for a beatoraja result skin and the POOR judgments alone (`judgedPoor`) beside an LR2 `score.poor` that also counts the empty POORs, so a BP read from it counts each empty POOR once. It is read from a play state (`playResultFromPlayState`) or from a course run (`playResultFromCourseRun`, `joinCourseStageHistories`), with how a run is presented (`resolveResultPresentation`).
- Course view: `CourseView` / `resolveCourseView` and the course combo (`createCourseCombo`, `reduceCourseCombo`, `advanceCourseCombo`).
- System sounds: `SystemSoundSet`, a theme's cues and jingles by slot, from an LR2 theme (`systemSoundSetFromLr2Theme`, `LR2_SOUND_SET_SLOTS`) or a beatoraja theme (`systemSoundSetFromBeatoraja`), with the result jingles a run plays (`resolveResultJingles`, `resolveResultJingle`) and the cue a browse command or a start plays (`browseSystemSound`, `startSystemSound`).
