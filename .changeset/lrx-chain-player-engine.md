LRX chain notes play as plugout plays them: in a judged play, each press that sounds a note (an empty press included) plays the chain notes tied to it, each as long after the press as it follows the note in the chart, and without a press they stay silent. Before, they played in the background at their own times; AUTO play still does that. A replay sought past a press starts its chain notes partway, as it does the press's keysound.
