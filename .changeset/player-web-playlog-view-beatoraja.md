The beatoraja gameplay scene records what the player saw in its play logs: the hi-speed (on the `beatoraja` scale), the visible time at the main BPM (the white number) and the lane cover, lift and HIDDEN cover as fractions of the lane height, at the start and on every change from the keys, the wheel or the skin's cover buttons, coalesced to at most one per 100 ms.
