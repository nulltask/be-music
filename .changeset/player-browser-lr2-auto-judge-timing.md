A play takes the automatic judge-timing mode (`judgeTimingAutoAdjust`), including LR2's ON and SILENT. Under LR2 SILENT, as in LR2, the play loads none of the chart's keysounds, BGM or BGA and the built-in and LR2 play screens draw no judgment or combo; a replay follows the mode it recorded, reading an older replay's `true` as beatoraja's. An LR2 select skin's button 75 cycles OFF, ON and SILENT, and an LR2 play skin's number 12 shows the live judge timing (#777).
