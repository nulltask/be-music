Add the embed page `/embed/?track=<URL>`, a preview of a chart archive for iframes. A track with several charts lists them with each chart's `#STAGEFILE` (or its `#BANNER`), a single chart (or the one `&chart=<file name or path>` names) waits behind a play button, and the chart plays in AUTO with the lanes on the left and the BGA as large as fits beside them (above them on a tall screen), with the play status beside or under it. Video-player controls (a thin seek bar, mute with a volume slider, full screen, a click on the picture to play or pause, keyboard shortcuts) fade out with the pointer after 3 seconds without input during a play. A progress bar shows the download and the reading of the charts. Like a video, the preview plays its sound with an iPhone or iPad in silent mode. A play ends back on the list, or on a replay button.
LR2 and beatoraja course files inside the archive can be previewed with `course` and an optional `courseName`: EXPERT and GRADE stages play in order with carried gauge and combo, and LR2 NONSTOP courses are stitched as in the player.
