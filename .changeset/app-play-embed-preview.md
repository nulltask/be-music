Add the embed page `/embed/?track=<URL>`, a preview of a chart archive for iframes: a track with several charts lists them in a scrolling list, a single chart (or the one `&chart=<file name or path>` names) waits behind a play button, and the chart plays in AUTO with lanes on the left, the BGA as large as fits right of them with the play status beside or under it, and video-player controls (seek, volume, full screen, keyboard shortcuts). A progress bar shows the download and the reading of the charts. A play ends back on the list, or on a replay button.
