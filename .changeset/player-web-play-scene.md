Add `createPlayScene(kit, options)`, which makes the play screen of every family. A play session holds the play's behaviour around one scene-model `PlayState`: the chart with its lane options and `#RANDOM` draws, the media (opened through the host's audio service), the engine, the keys and touch lanes, the hi-speed, covers and play-log view, and the ending. The family's view draws the shared playfield and the skin around it: LR2 skins through `@be-music/lr2-skin/state`, beatoraja skins with their note, marker and BGA layers through `@be-music/beatoraja-skin/scene`. Load the scene with `prepare(onProgress)` (off stage, behind a decide screen), show it with `PixiSceneHost.setScene` and begin it with `start()`. `preResolved` plays the branches the decide screen drew (`DecideScene.resolved`), and `resolved` is the chart as played, for the result's graphs.

`PlayScene` also has `requestExit()`, `requestTogglePause()` / `isPaused()`, `setTempo()` / `getTempo()`, `setHiSpeed()` / `getHiSpeed()`, `recording`, `progress()` (the result so far), `waitForPlaylog()`, `updateKit()`, the audio mix and drawing setters, and `dispose({ preserveAudioTail })`. `on.finish(result, playlog)` hands over a `PlayResult`, which now carries the gauge's clear border and the engine's TOTAL (`gaugeBorder`, `gaugeTotal`) and each judgment's early / late count (`earlyLate`) on every family. A beatoraja play now takes the session's ESC fade-out over the skin's `fadeout`, F5 restart, Space pause (its skin timers included) and auto-pause, steps the hi-speed once per arrow key press, and plays the skin's Lua sounds through the audio service.

Migration:

- `new DefaultPixiGameplayView({ beMusicSkin, beMusicEffects, beMusicSkinSettings, layoutMode, onExit, onRestart, onChartFinished, ...settings })`, then `view.mount(host, song, source)` (or `prepare(host, song, source)` and `start()`) → `createPlayScene({ family: 'built-in', skin: beMusicSkin, effects: beMusicEffects, settings: beMusicSkinSettings }, { chart: song, source, settings: { layoutMode, ...settings }, audio, host, on: { finish: (result, playlog) => onChartFinished(result), exit: onExit, restart: onRestart } })`, then `await scene.prepare()`, `await host.setScene(scene)` and `scene.start()`, where `audio` is the host's `createAudioService()`.
- `new PixiGameplayView({ skin, invisibleNoteSkin, … })` → `createPlayScene({ family: 'lr2', skin, invisibleNoteSkin }, { … })` as above.
- `prepareBeatorajaGameplayChart({ song, source, … })`, then `new PixiBeatorajaGameplayView({ skin, textures, fonts, skinConfig, variant, chart, audio, mode, dpFlip, engineOptions, bgaTextures, bgaVideoElements, chartImages, bgaCues, skinAudio, onExit, onComplete })` → `createPlayScene({ family: 'beatoraja', kit }, { chart: song, source, settings, audio, host, on: { finish, exit: onExit, restart } })`, where `kit` is `loadBeatorajaSceneKit`'s for the chart's variant, `settings` carries what went into `mode`, `dpFlip` and `engineOptions` (`autoPlay`, `dpFlip`, the judge ruleset, the gauge), and `audio` has the theme's files set (`audio.setTheme({ files })`) for the skin's Lua sounds. The scene loads the chart's samples, BGA and images itself; `directoryLabel`, `chartPath`, `inputTarget`, `shouldSkipKey` and `onError` have no counterpart.
- `onComplete(summary, maxCombo, history)` → `on.finish(result, playlog)`; `view.getPlaylog()` / `awaitCompletion()` → the `playlog` of `on.finish`, or `scene.waitForPlaylog()` after an exit.
- `view.getResultData()` → `scene.progress()`.
- `view.setBeMusicSkinSettings(settings)` → `scene.updateKit({ ...kit, settings })`; `view.replaceSkin(…)` → `scene.updateKit({ family: 'beatoraja', kit })`.
- `view.startRecording()` / `stopRecording()` / `isRecording()` → `scene.recording.startRecording()` / `stopRecording()` / `isRecording()`.
- `skinlessChromeRenderer` has no counterpart: draw the chrome with a be-music skin through a built-in kit.
