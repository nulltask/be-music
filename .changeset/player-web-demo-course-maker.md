Make your own EXPERT, NONSTOP and GRADE courses on the select screen, as on LR2: decide NEW COURSE in a course folder, decide songs to add them, then save. A card shows the course being made on every skin (LR2 skins also show it in their own course panel), Debug Menu → **Course: my courses…** renames, edits NONSTOP connections, deletes and exports the courses as `.lr2crs`, and the courses are kept in IndexedDB.
