Make your own EXPERT, NONSTOP and GRADE courses on the select screen, as on LR2: decide NEW COURSE in a course folder, decide charts to add them, then save. A card shows the course being made on every skin, and LR2 skins also show it in their own course panel. Set constraints on your courses as beatoraja's course editor does (lane option, hi-speed lock, judge, gauge, long-note mode). Debug Menu → Course → My courses… renames them, edits NONSTOP connections, deletes them and exports them as `.lr2crs` or as a beatoraja course `.json` that keeps the constraints. The courses are kept in IndexedDB.
