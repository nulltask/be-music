Play each LRX note with its own volume, balance, rate, sample window and fade-in / fade-out. `WebAudioSessionSlicePlayback` gains `gain`, `pan`, `rate`, `attackSeconds` and `releaseSeconds`; seeks, keep-pitch tempo and the result screen's audio tail follow them, and the select-screen preview applies the volume and rate. LRX background sounds that plugout mixes with the keysounds (chain notes, hidden notes and most background columns) play at the keysound volume.
