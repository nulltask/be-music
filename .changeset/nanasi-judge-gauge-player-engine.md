nanasigroove's GOOD2BAD and GREAT2BAD keep every judgment and move the gauge as a BAD instead of collapsing the judge windows, HELLJUDGE narrows each window by nanasigroove's own ratio (PGREAT and GREAT 3/8, GOOD 1/3, BAD 1/4) instead of halving it, and ADDMINE places nanasigroove's mines through the new `NANASI_ADD` mine option. `PlayerOptions.playerChartPlayOptions` lets a player select GOOD2BAD / GREAT2BAD and HELLJUDGE. Replays recorded before this keep their earlier readings.
