Press R on the result screen to play the same chart again at once, skipping the select and decide screens. The retry keeps the play's mode (AUTO, or a replay of the same play log) and uses the current play options. F5 during a play now also starts the chart again in the play's own mode: an AUTO play started from a skin's AUTO button came back as a manual play, and a replay came back as a live play that could write a best record, because the restart followed the Debug Menu's Auto play.
