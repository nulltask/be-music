The play screens draw the rest of the chart-authored display options. Notes take STAR colours, SPIN rotation, CLONE afterimages and the Y / XY / 3D waves. REVERSE mirrors the note stream and HIDJUDGE hides the judgment display. DARK hides the BGA, and DARK2 the bombs as well. LINEMOVE raises the judgement line through LIFT on views that draw it. M-SUDDEN shows the player's SUDDEN+ cover, which F3 and F4 move. LR2 and beatoraja skins keep their own judgement line under REVERSE, and LR2 skins, which have no LIFT, ignore LINEMOVE.
