Add `createSelectScene(kit, options)` to `@be-music/player-web/scenes`: the select screen built on the scene session and family view contracts (ADR 0001). Its session browses the library with the browse model of `@be-music/scene-model` and the family's select policy, keeps the play options and the option panels, shows a chart's text file, and starts a chart or a course (`on.start(entry, { autoPlay })`, after a be-music skin's launch outro). It plays through the audio service: the select BGM, ducked under the chart preview, and the preview of the chart under the cursor are the session's own and stop as the screen leaves the stage, while the cues and the decide jingle (`sounds.decideBgm`) have no owner and play out, so the jingle rings on through the decide screen into the play. Coming back with the cursor on the chart it left on does not start that chart's preview again, as before. The built-in family's view draws a be-music skin's `select` screen and turns taps, swipes, the wheel and the skin's hit areas into intents.

The scene is a `SessionScene` to show with `PixiSceneHost.setScene`, kept across the screens a play goes through. `SelectScene` adds `snapshot()`, `focused()`, `getPlayOptions()` / `setPlayOptions()`, `setLibrary()`, `restore()`, `refreshRecords()`, `setSearch()` / `setSearchEditing()`, `searchFieldRect()`, `setSounds()` and `updateKit()`. `CoreSongSelectView` (and so `PixiSongSelectView`) answers to the same names, so a host drives both alike.

`SelectFrame` and `SelectIntent` change shape: the frame carries the events since the last frame (`events`: browse events, option panels, README scrolls) in place of `lastBrowse`, a `revision`, the README shown (`readText`) and the library's files, and drops `focusedCourse`; the intents gain `activate`, `set-play-option` and `gesture`, and `start` plays the bar under the cursor without opening a folder. `SelectView` adds `launchOutroMs` and `searchFieldRect()`.
