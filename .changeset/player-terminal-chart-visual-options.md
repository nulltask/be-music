The terminal grid applies chart-authored display options: CHANGE-HS and the Y waves move notes along the lane, notes a HIDDEN option makes less than half opaque are hidden, REVERSE flips the grid so the judge row is on top, HIDJUDGE blanks the judge line, and DARK hides the BGA. The sprite-only CRAZY effects and M-SUDDEN have no grid form and are not drawn.
