Play LR2 NONSTOP courses as one stitched chart, with the steps in `@be-music/player/course` shared by the hosts and the Leaderboard:

- `.lr2crs` NONSTOP courses carry their connections (`CourseDefinition.connections`, `parseLR2NonstopConnections`), and `planNonstopCourse` places the stages one after another as LR2's chart loader stitches them (last-measure handover, cutting, FIT time-stretch, BLANK gaps), with `createNonstopStageTiming` reading a stage chart.
- `stitchNonstopCourse` turns the placed stages into one BMS chart that plays as LR2's NONSTOP loader builds it: a time-based 150-BPM scroll with every object at its stage's own time, each stage's bar lines and STOPs in its window, LR2's seam rule turning overlapping notes into background sound, and keys renumbered into one base-62 space. Its map gives each stage's keys, window, sound fades and BGA crossfade, the BPM readout (`tempo`: each stage's own BPM scaled by its FIT rate, as LR2 shows it; `resolveNonstopTempo`, `nonstopBPMAt`), and each later stage's `#RANK` (`stageRanks`, `resolveNonstopStageRanks`), which the engine takes as the `stageRanks` play option, the LR2 ruleset applies, and the play log records as `chart.judgeRank.stageRanks`.
- The `lr2` placement follows the LR2 binary exactly: its third-stage start offset, the BPM readout in LR2's event order, and the rank switch at each stage's own time zero, a stage without `#RANK` keeping the rank in force, with LR2's own NONSTOP window table (wider than a chart's opening table for VERY HARD and HARD). The default `'intended'` placement reads the BPM over each stage's window, switches the rank halfway between the two stages' notes to the windows a chart opening with that `#RANK` gets, and reads a stage without `#RANK` as NORMAL.
- `prepareNonstopCourse` resolves each stage chart's `#RANDOM` / `#SWITCH` (with recorded draws, or drawn), applies the course's long-note constraint and stitches the stages, with the host's asset plumbing coming in through `assetPath`. `resolveNonstopRun` gives a play's `nonstop` record (the stage placements with their draws and the stage of every note), and `buildNonstopCoursePlaylog` builds the run's course play log, naming each stage by its chart fingerprint.
- The stitching rules are versioned (`NONSTOP_STITCH_VERSION`, recorded as `nonstop.stitch` in a course play log), so a stitched chart can be rebuilt by the rules it was played on after the rules change. `stitchNonstopCourse` takes the version as `options.stitch` and refuses one it does not know (`stitch-version`); a log without it reads as version 1.
