`@be-music/skin-lr2/state` adds `stepLR2LaneOption`, the lane option a click on an LR2 RANDOM button (42 / 43) moves to: the next of LR2's own six values, stepping on from another source's RANDOM, S-RANDOM or CONVERGE by its name and moving any other value to OFF (ADR 0019). The RANDOM buttons draw SCATTER and CONVERGE on cells 4 and 5.
