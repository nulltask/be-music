Fix long notes judging differently from LR2 and beatoraja, and between live play and play-log re-simulations, which now share the ruleset's long-note rules:

- An LN released just before its tail scored a BAD: LR2 keeps the head judgment unless the release comes more than the GOOD window early, and beatoraja takes the worse of the head and the release judged against the long-note-end windows.
- Releases waited 120 ms before they settled, so a re-press within that time (or a key chatter) continued a long note that LR2 and beatoraja end at the release. With key-state input a release now settles at once, except under the ruleset's `longNoteReleaseMarginUs` (beatoraja's 200 ms `longnoteMargin` on PMS keys), where a BAD-or-worse early release waits and a re-press resumes the hold. Only an early release is held back, taking the head's timing when it was further off, so a release after a late BAD head settles at once, as in beatoraja.
- A second key pressing a lane whose long note is held (such as the turntable's other direction) judged the lane's next note and dropped the held note's judgment. It now follows the ruleset's `pressDuringLongNote` rule: LR2 judges the LN head again inside its BAD window and charges an empty POOR otherwise; beatoraja and IIDX ignore it.
- An unpressed LN head under the LR2 ruleset was missed only after its late BAD window closed: LR2 has no late BAD on an LN head and misses it once time moves past the late GOOD leg (OpenLR2 `ProcLongnote`). The simulator also no longer skips such a miss when a plain note just ahead of it on the same lane still has time left.
- A charge note (CN / HCN) held past its tail's late window scored a BAD instead of a missed POOR when the window closed on a replay step exactly on its edge.
- Hell Charge notes ran a raw 2.5 %/s from the press in live play, with a released HCN kept open until its tail, while the simulator ended the ticks at the release. Both now run beatoraja's 200 ms ticks through the gauge's judge table (GREAT ×0.5 gaining, BAD ×0.5 draining) from the judged head until the tail passes, gaining while a key is down on the lane or after a GOOD-or-better tail, and judge the tail at the release like a charge note. Only the HCN crossing the judge line ticks, so one pressed early no longer loses its ticks to a later miss of the HCN ahead of it.
- AUTO SCRATCH scored a charge note's head and tail together (at the tail in the engine, at the head in the simulator), so a miss in between broke the combo differently: both now clear the head at the head and the tail at the tail, as beatoraja's autoplay does.
