Play NONSTOP courses as one stitched chart, as LR2 does, on every skin family's own play skin, with each stage's FIT rate, seam fades, BGA and judge rank; the course's lane-option constraint applies to the stitched play. As LR2 has no course-result step for NONSTOP, the run shows on the result skin with the clear / fail jingle, and R on it stitches and plays the course again, skipping the decide screen. Its best record is kept per connections and placement mode, as its course fingerprint is, and changing Debug Menu → Course → NONSTOP placement redraws the select screen's lamp and trophy at once. A theme without a result skin falls back to the built-in result with the theme's own jingles.
