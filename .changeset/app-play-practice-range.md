Aiming PRACTICE at a passage no longer means guessing millisecond numbers: Debug Menu's PRACTICE folder shows the note-density graph of the chart under the cursor with a start and an end handle to drag (mouse or touch). The handles snap to measure starts, the caption shows the range's times (m:ss.s) and measure numbers, and START TIME / END TIME follow the handles and the other way round. The picker shows only while PRACTICE is on and is disabled during a play like the other PRACTICE settings (#948).
