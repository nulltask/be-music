The new `applyChartLaneCovers` returns the lane covers to draw under a chart's M-SUDDEN and LINEMOVE. M-SUDDEN shows the player's SUDDEN+ cover at its own height, and LINEMOVE adds to the LIFT up to `CHART_LINE_LIFT_MAX`. The player's covers themselves are left unchanged.
