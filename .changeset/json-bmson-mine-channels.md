Add `BmsonMineNoteEntry` / `BmsonMineChannelEntry` for the beatoraja bmson extension `mine_channels`, which holds bmson mines and their `damage`. `BmsonKeyNoteEntry` now documents `key_channels` as beatoraja's hidden notes, and its `l`, `c` and `damage` fields are deprecated because nothing reads them.
