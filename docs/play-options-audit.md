# Play option coverage and assist policies

## Scope and evidence

This audit compares the play options of LR2, beatoraja and That-Which-Must-Not-Be-Named with Be-Music. The inspection date is **2026-10-24**, and the Be-Music snapshot is **`424857f648b56dc1d1e06330bebeaefe18bcf3d1`**. Findings come from source and documentation inspection, not original-machine runtime testing. It covers options that affect chart arrangement, judging, gauges, scrolling, presentation and practice; account customization, song selection filters and cosmetic skin inventories are outside the comparison.

The reference versions are:

- **LR2 beta3 100201:** the original distribution's `help/4.html`, `LR2files/Help/playoption.txt`, `LR2files/Help/systemoption.txt`, `LR2files/Config/optionstr.csv` and `history.txt`. The locally inspected archive is `LR2_100201.zip`. The historical source reference is OpenLR2's `LR2Beta3-v100201`, commit [`583a38503555e59c4bafa49d60b9d0714c9007c2`](https://github.com/GOMazk/OpenLR2/tree/583a38503555e59c4bafa49d60b9d0714c9007c2). Its reconstructed source has known TODOs; the original help and history take precedence when they explain a feature. Features added by current OpenLR2 are outside this baseline.
- **beatoraja 0.8.9:** released on 2026-10-21; source commit [`83574048ba489082eb46df7fe9806b464bf1f526`](https://github.com/exch-bms2/beatoraja/tree/83574048ba489082eb46df7fe9806b464bf1f526). This was also the upstream `master` head when inspected. The tables below distinguish launcher choices from internal enum values.
- **That-Which-Must-Not-Be-Named:** the current **AC 34** official option pages, plus the PC edition's official additional-option page. Historical AC features are listed separately. AC and PC recording policies are kept separate. The [official release announcement](https://www.konami.com/arcadegames/corporate/ja/topics/2026/0916/) establishes the current AC generation.

The browser's skin family and judge ruleset are separate choices. Loading an LR2 or beatoraja skin does not supply that game's complete option menu or change the engine's recording policy. Most currently available Be-Music choices are shared by all skin families.

### Reading the tables

- **Supported:** the option can be selected and its relevant playback behavior is implemented. This does not certify every original game's algorithm or controls.
- **Partial:** implementation exists in a resolver, replay format, a particular view or a restricted execution path, while normal selection or the original behavior remains incomplete.
- **Unavailable:** new plays cannot select the option, including deliberately disabled legacy values.
- **Unconfirmed:** the inspected primary material does not establish availability or recording policy. An omitted item on an official page is not proof that it was removed.
- **Assist** describes the original game's treatment. A gentler gauge, an input assist, a chart modifier and a practice mode can have different score and lamp policies.

A skin operation number, configuration string, parser capability or replay field alone does not establish a completed option.

## Conditions that change assist classification

The beatoraja source determines assist status from the actual execution path, sometimes from the chart's content. The following distinctions are relevant to any future implementation:

- Ordinary MIRROR/RANDOM/ROTATE/S-RANDOM/FLIP remain unassisted. SPIRAL, H-RANDOM, ALL-SCR, the exposed EX arrangements and the PMS playable arrangements declare LIGHT_ASSIST. [B2]
- BPM GUIDE requires a BPM-changing chart before it marks the play assisted. CUSTOM JUDGE requires an enabled rate above 100% in one of its tested judgment or LN-margin fields. Narrowing a window alone does not trigger that test. [B3]
- Mine removal and scroll removal mark LIGHT_ASSIST when the chart actually contains the removed material. LN removal, charge-note addition and extra-note conversion similarly mark ASSIST when the relevant transformation occurs. Mine addition, scroll addition and ADD LN do not independently set an assist flag in their modifiers. Changes in difficulty therefore do not, by themselves, establish the original's assist class. [B4]
- `PlayerBattleModifier` has an ASSIST declaration, while `BMSPlayer`'s actual BATTLE construction explicitly applies LIGHT_ASSIST. The latter execution path determines the resulting play. [B2] [B3]
- `PlayConfig.enableConstant` is a rendering setting. `PlayerConfig.scrollMode=REMOVE` rewrites the chart and participates in assist aggregation. They need different option identities and policies. [B3] [B4] [B6]

The inspected beatoraja launcher and `PlayerConfig` do not establish a standalone AUTO SCRATCH selector for normal play. `BATTLE AS` is confirmed. LR2 skin compatibility controls in Be-Music are not evidence that upstream beatoraja exposes an identical setting. [B1] [B3]

## Recording policy: original behavior and current Be-Music behavior

### Original games

LR2's original help explicitly says AUTO SCRATCH prevents score saving and yields EASY CLEAR. Its history also establishes restrictions for SCATTER and CONVERGE, and separate lamps for converted/special play modes. The original distribution contains inconsistent descriptions of SP-TO-DP saving between older help files; its later history is the stronger source. The reconstructed source also has TODO comments around side-specific assist checks, so unsupported edge cases should remain qualified. [L1] [L2]

In beatoraja, `assist=1/2` selects `LightAssistEasy` or `AssistEasy` for a qualifying single-chart clear. The play also sets `updateScore=false`. This disables normal best-score-related updates; it does not mean every database write is discarded. `PlayDataAccessor.updateScore` considers a better clear separately from EX score, min-BP, timing and combo updates, which are gated by `updateScore`. [B3] [B5]

AC34 explicitly identifies AUTO SCRATCH, LEGACY and their combination as assist features, and explicitly says BATTLE saves no score data. The cited official pages do not fully specify every assist's score/lamp precedence. Keep unverified details open instead of importing another version's behavior. The PC edition explicitly says its four listed options produce NO PLAY and save no data. [A1] [P1]

### Be-Music at `424857f6`

- The Leaderboard uses `auto-scratch` and `judge-window-override` flags to rank assisted plays separately. Auto play, an aborted play, another tempo, an individual course stage and a replay without the required fingerprints are ineligible for the ordinary chart ranking. Course submission has its own eligibility path. [I7]
- Local `BestRecord` and its storage key separate charts/courses and judge rulesets, without an assist class. `playKeepsRecord` handles auto/replay/course/tempo distinctions, but does not exclude a manual assist merely because it uses AUTO SCRATCH. Local assisted results can therefore affect the same best-record slot. [I6]
- `CLEAR_LAMPS` includes `ASSIST_EASY` for a gentler-gauge clear. It has no separate light-assist/input-assist categories. The original game's cap and normal best-record restrictions are not reproduced by the existing IR separation. [I5] [I6] [I7]
- Web AUTO SCRATCH selectors are independent controls, but `autoScratch1P || autoScratch2P` is passed to the engine. This loses side-specific intent for DP. [I3]
- SCATTER is already blocked for new plays while placement and recording policy remain incomplete. Old replays can still reproduce their recorded resolved lanes. [I1] [I2]

These are findings, not a decision to migrate existing records, change lamps or reject assisted submissions. Those changes need an explicit compatibility and data policy.

## Follow-up work, without policy decisions

The clearest coverage gaps are rotation and additional arrangements, full gauge selection, constant-scroll behavior, non-beatoraja LIFT, timing calibration, LN-mode selection and the assist modifiers. Existing calculation tables can reduce implementation work, but each new option still needs selection, playback, replay serialization, verification and record-policy coverage.

Before introducing further assists, specify independently: the assist class, whether a score is accepted, which best-record fields may update, the earned lamp, the ranking partition, and any effect on course results. Existing local records and replay schemas require their own migration decision. This audit does not choose those policies. Existing follow-up issues are [#602](https://github.com/nulltask/be-music/issues/602) (assist/record policy), [#603](https://github.com/nulltask/be-music/issues/603) (lamp vocabulary and stored ranks), and [#604](https://github.com/nulltask/be-music/issues/604) (player LN mode and record identity).
